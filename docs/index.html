VFit

A weight-loss tracker that works out what you actually burn — from your own scale and food log — and tells you whether your metabolism is slowing down.

  • Your real TDEE — learned from your weight trend and intake, not a generic formula
  • Metabolic adaptation — how much your burn has dropped beyond what the weight you lost explains
  • Do next — how you've managed so far, and one concrete change: eat this much less, walk this much more, or hold steady
  • Forecasts & plateaus — where your weight will settle, 4/12/26-week projections, goal date, stall detection
  • Your day as a timeline — check-in, meals, snacks, exercise and 2-hour blood sugar, in order
  • Saved meals — search-as-you-type picker; log a usual meal in two taps
  • Fasting days — mark a fast and still log soups and juices
  • The year at a glance — every day coloured by what won: exercise, diet, fasting, or a day you lived a little — with targets, so you can see how much living is left
  • Trends you can scrub — status tiles and charts; slide a finger across any chart to read that day's values
  • Blood sugar & blood pressure — fasting and post-meal glucose, BP and pulse alongside weight
  • Bring your spreadsheet — CSV import, and export of everything any time
  • 100% local — no account, no sync, no tracking

Do next: how you're doing, and what to change

Most trackers stop at charts. VFit's Do next tab tells you how to evaluate your health management so far — and what to do about it.

How it works

Calorie calculators guess your expenditure from age, height and weight. VFit measures it instead: if you logged eating 2,000 kcal a day for a month and your weight trend held steady, you burn about 2,000 kcal a day — in your logging units, so consistent under-logging is absorbed rather than throwing the numbers off.

1. Log a little every day Morning weight, what you ate (saved meals make it quick), and optionally waist, exercise, blood sugar and BP. Missed a day? It's filled from the days around it and shown as assumed.
2. A filter separates signal from noise A Kalman filter tracks your true weight through day-to-day water swings and learns your expenditure as it goes. Each estimate uses only the data up to that day.
3. You get answers, not just graphs Your TDEE with a confidence range, adaptation since you started, where you'll settle at current intake, whether you've plateaued and the likely reason — and what to change.

Installing the APK

VFit is not on the Google Play Store. Install it directly — this needs installs from unknown sources enabled. Android 8.0 or newer.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap "Download APK" above, or get vfit.apk from the Releases page.

  2. Allow installs from unknown sources

    When prompted after opening the APK, tap Settings → enable "Allow from this source".

  3. Open the APK and install

    Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Downloads folder. Tap Install.

  4. Set up and start logging

    In Settings, enter your height, age and goal. Log tomorrow morning's weight and your meals. Estimates start after about three weeks; already have a spreadsheet? Use Import CSV.

About this app

VFit is local only. Your weight, meals and health readings stay on your device. No account required. No data leaves your phone. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

It started as a spreadsheet and a Python script to answer one question during a weight-loss program: is my metabolism slowing down more than the weight I've lost explains? The app runs the same model on your phone.

The code was written by Claude Code. The intent, design, and product decisions were made by a human. The animation above uses made-up demo data.
